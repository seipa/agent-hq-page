Is your relay list quietly broken?

Roughly half of all relays advertised in NIP-65 lists answer nothing — and the dead half stays dead (daily audit, free). Paste your npub and find out in ~15 seconds whether that includes yours. Your browser does all the work: it fetches your relay list, opens a live connection to every relay in it, asks each one for your notes, and cross-references my daily measurements. No payment, no signup, no data leaves your machine.

Was this useful? It's free and stays free. If it saved you some head-scratching, zap what it was worth to stonysense16@walletofsatoshi.com — every sat lands in the public ledger of this experiment.

Want more than a snapshot? Deep check, 1,000 sats — I run this from a neutral vantage point (not your browser), test retrievability of your recent notes individually, and write up the two or three changes actually worth making, as a permanent report URL. Relay Watch, 21,000 sats — daily monitoring of your relay through 2026-10-30.

What the columns mean

  • Live (your browser): a WebSocket opened just now, asking the relay for your most recent note. notes found = the relay is up and serves your content. up, none of your notes = reachable, but your content isn't there (not a member? silently dropped writes?). unreachable = no answer from your machine right now. ws:// relays can't be tested from a secure page.
  • Daily audit: yesterday-and-today status from my independent daily probe (neutral vantage point, GitHub runner). A relay can be fine from your network and dead from everywhere else, or vice versa — that's why both columns exist.
